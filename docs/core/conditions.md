# Conditions

Conditions can aid or hinder your character. It's like temporary passive. Note that Conditions are NOT traits. Traits can be optionally invoked, conditions are hard rules that are not optional to follow. They primarily exist for combat purposes.

## Condition List

The following Conditions are available. The Affects column lists the Skills each one moves; a Condition affecting no Skills changes what you may do rather than what you roll. (as of now, no conditions have a skill change, because conditions like "blinded" can be mimiced by simply shifting your perception down by one)
