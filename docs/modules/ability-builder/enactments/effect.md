## Enact Effect

The Enact Effect applies a lingering effect to a target, such as fire, frost, or poison damage. By default, the effect lasts for {{fieldDefault (enactment "persistent_effect") "duration"}} rounds and triggers at the start of the target's turn. On the target's turn they can re-roll the solution to get rid of the effect or take an action to remove it.
